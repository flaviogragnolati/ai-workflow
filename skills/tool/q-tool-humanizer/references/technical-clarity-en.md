# STE-informed technical clarity reference

Load this reference only with `clarity-en.md` for English `improve` work routed to `clarity_profile: ste-informed-technical`. It adapts general structural ideas from ASD-STE100 Issue 9 for technical ambiguity. It does not contain the controlled dictionary. It does not verify every ASD-STE100 rule. It does not produce certified or compliant documentation.

The meaning lock in `SKILL.md` governs every edit. Treat the limits below as review signals, never permission to delete or strengthen content. Record each necessary exception in `profile_departures`.

## Terminology and direct syntax

- `C1`: Make the actor visible and use a direct verb when the source identifies the actor. Keep passive voice when the actor is unknown, irrelevant, protected, or not the sentence focus.
- `C3`: Use one brief, common term for the same actor, object, state, or action when no authorized term exists. Preserve project, company, industry, and defined terminology.
- `C6`: Reuse the selected term instead of rotating synonyms. Keep subjects, verbs, and articles when omission creates more than one reading. Resolve a pronoun only from supplied evidence.
- `C6`: Review noun groups longer than three words. Clarify their relationships with prepositions, relative clauses, or an already defined short name; do not alter an official technical name.
- `C3`: Replace a phrasal verb only when one direct verb has exactly the same meaning in context. Never claim that a selected word belongs to the official ASD-STE100 dictionary.
- `C6`: Avoid contractions in high-precision instructions when the expanded form makes polarity, modality, or the actor easier to parse.

## Procedures and descriptions

For a procedure:

- `C1`: Use the imperative when the reader performs the action.
- `C7`: Put one instruction in each sentence. Keep actions together when they must occur simultaneously; separating them would change the operation.
- `C8`: Put a condition first when the reader must know it before acting.
- `C8`: Keep each limit, tolerance, required result, and consequence beside the action it controls.

For a description:

- `C7`: Keep one principal subject or topic in each sentence and one topic in each paragraph.
- `C7`: Present information gradually, from the context the reader needs to the dependent detail.
- `C6`: Use an explicit connector when it carries supplied cause, contrast, sequence, or result.
- `C1`: Do not convert a description into an instruction unless the caller authorizes that change of function.

## Mechanical review signals

- `C9`: Review a procedural sentence above 20 words and a descriptive sentence above 25 words.
- `C7`: Review a paragraph above six sentences.
- `C7`: Use a vertical list when it makes a sequence or complex enumeration easier to parse.
- `C9`: Avoid semicolons in this profile; split or reconnect the sentence only when the relation stays exact.

If a sentence must remain longer to preserve a condition, quotation, defined term, measurement, or simultaneous action, keep it and record why. The meaning lock always prevails.

## Verb time and modality

- `C6`: Prefer a simple tense only when it preserves the exact time, aspect, and current relevance.
- `C6`: Keep the present perfect, an auxiliary, a modal, or an `-ing` form when it carries information that another form would lose.
- `C6`: Never change `may have failed` to `failed`. Preserve every degree of possibility, obligation, permission, and prohibition.
- `C6`: Record a deliberate tense or form exception in `profile_departures`.

## Safety information

- `C8`: Check that a safety statement makes its supplied command or controlling condition visible before dependent information.
- `C8`: Keep the supplied consequence or possible result visible and associated with that command or condition.
- `C8`: Do not invent or upgrade a risk category, hazard, harm, probability, or outcome. When the source lacks information needed to clarify the safety statement, preserve it and route the gap to the content owner.

## Coverage and completion

This profile is a general adaptation of structural principles. It does not include the ASD-STE100 controlled dictionary, assess every ASD-STE100 rule, or certify compliance. Actual compliance requires the official current standard, its controlled vocabulary, and review by a qualified professional.

Complete the profile pass when every edit belongs to exactly one `C` family, terminology is consistent, procedural and descriptive functions remain intact, ambiguous structures are resolved only from supplied meaning, every mechanical exception is recorded, safety gaps are owner-routed, and `standards_compliance` is `not-assessed`.
