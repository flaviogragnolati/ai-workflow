### Deliberately not incorporated

Quasar does not copy the upstream frontmatter, always-loaded capability summary, visual-aid/OpenRouter route, generic scientific-process tutorial, exhaustive catalogs, output prose, examples, peer-review overlap, or unrestricted write-tool declaration. It does not treat evidence hierarchy, GRADE, Cochrane tools, reporting guidelines, or clinical instruments as universally applicable.

### Quasar modifications

- Made the capability read-only with no persistent output, target mutation, open investigation, workflow-state write, publication, or certification.
- Added an explicit caller boundary so Research, Technical Research, and Proposal Discovery retain confidence, synthesis, registration, follow-up, and readiness decisions.
- Split detailed criteria into five conditionally loaded references and kept the scientific/clinical branch out of general business and engineering reviews.
- Added bounded URL reads, untrusted-content handling, honest unreadable-source fallback, and transient diagnostics with stable findings.
- Added general business and engineering coverage for benchmarks, vendor claims, KPIs, ML/AI evaluation, incidents, surveys, and case studies.
